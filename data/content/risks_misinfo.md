# The Risks: Misinformation, Bias, and Deepfakes

AI tools can quickly generate highly convincing but false or biased content. 

## Key Risks:
1.  **Misinformation/Deepfakes:** Realistic fake images and videos are easy to create, making it hard to trust what we see.
2.  **Algorithmic Bias:** AI models can reflect biases in their training data, leading to unfair or incomplete outputs.
3.  **Inappropriate Outputs:** Without proper filters, children may encounter harmful or explicit content.

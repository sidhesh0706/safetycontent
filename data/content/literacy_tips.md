# Digital Literacy and Monitoring: The Ultimate Defense

The best defense against the risks of AI-generated content is an educated and empowered user. Digital literacy is an essential life skill in the modern world.

## Tips for Parents and Educators:

* **Encourage Scrutiny:** Teach children to question the source of information, especially if it seems shocking or too perfect.
* **Use Watermarks/Metadata:** Look for signs that content was AI-generated (e.g., watermarks, strange artifacts).
* **Establish Clear Rules:** Set family or classroom guidelines on when and how AI tools can be used for schoolwork.

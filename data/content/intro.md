# Welcome to the AI Safety Guide

The rapid growth of AI-generated text, images, and videos necessitates a comprehensive guide to ensure content authenticity and safety for young users.

## Project Goal

This project aims to:
1. Promote awareness and responsible use of artificial intelligence tools.
2. Provide educators and parents with tools to monitor AI usage.
3. Foster critical thinking and digital literacy in children.

We believe that by combining research, awareness campaigns, and interactive learning, we can empower families and educators to create a safe and informed digital environment.
